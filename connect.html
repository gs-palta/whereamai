Where Am aI Publisher

The publisher posts finished Where Am aI episodes to the series' own TikTok account through the TikTok Content Posting API. It is used only by the channel operator.

  1. Connect the TikTok account below and approve the requested permissions (basic profile info, video upload and publish).
  2. The callback page shows an authorization code, which the operator's local publishing tool exchanges for an access token.
  3. The tool uploads each finished episode with its caption and privacy setting, then confirms the post status.
