Where Am aI

Where was this taken? Solved clue by clue. Think you know a spot it can't get? Send it.

Where Am aI is a short-form video series about finding places. Each episode starts from a few panorama views of an unknown spot, with no address and no map pin, and follows the search step by step until the exact location is found.

How an episode works

  1. The drop. A searcher gets a handful of panorama views from somewhere in the world: a street, a harbour, a bedroom.
  2. The budget. Every extra action costs from a small budget: zooming in, opening a map, searching for a place, checking satellite imagery.
  3. The clues. Signs, plants, road markings, architecture and shop names narrow the search from continent to country to street.
  4. The solve. The episode ends at the exact spot.

Some episodes are quizzes instead: three clues from one room or street, and you guess before the reveal.
