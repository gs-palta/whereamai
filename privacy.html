Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-21

The Where Am aI publishing tool ("the Tool") is used solely by its owner to publish video content to the owner's own Instagram and TikTok accounts.

The Tool does not collect, store, or process personal data of any third party. It does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking of any kind.

The only data handled by the Tool are platform access tokens for the owner's own accounts, stored locally on the owner's machine and used exclusively to publish content via the official Instagram Graph API and TikTok Content Posting API.

No data is shared with or sold to anyone.
